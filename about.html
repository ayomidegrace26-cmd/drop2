We Are Drop.

Born in Nigeria. Built for Africa. Driven by Purpose.

We didn't just build an app, we built a movement.

Drop was born out of a simple observation: Nigerians are incredibly resourceful, yet often constrained by fragmented services and unreliable infrastructure. We saw artisans struggling to find consistent work, commuters battling unpredictable transport, and businesses hampered by inefficient logistics.

We envisioned a unified platform that could solve these challenges holistically. Not just a ride-hailing app, but a comprehensive ecosystem that connects verified professionals, reliable transport, and secure logistics under one trusted umbrella.

Today, Drop is more than a technology company; we are an enabler of progress. Our ambition is to be Africa's most trusted mobility and services platform, empowering millions of individuals and businesses to move freely, work efficiently, and thrive in an interconnected economy.

Our Mission

To empower Nigerians with a unified, reliable, and accessible platform that seamlessly connects them to essential services, transportation, and economic opportunities. We strive to simplify daily life while fostering community growth.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted and versatile mobility ecosystem, driving innovation and socioeconomic development across the continent. We envision a future where distance and access are no longer barriers to progress.

What We Stand For

The core principles that guide our decisions, shape our culture, and define the Drop experience.

Innovation

We constantly push boundaries to create smarter, more efficient solutions that adapt to the unique needs of our users.

Community

We believe in collective progress. Our success is measured by the tangible impact we create within the communities we serve.

Safety

Trust is our currency. We prioritize the physical and digital security of every user, partner, and transaction on our platform.

Reliability

We deliver on our promises. Whether it's a ride, a package, or a service, you can count on Drop to get it done right.
